# Brand Voice Guide

Owner: marketing@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/brand/voice
Last revised: 19 September 2026

## Tone

Write plainly and directly. Avoid exclamation marks.

Northwind sounds like someone who knows the answer and respects your time. Prefer
the second person. Short sentences. Contractions are fine.

## Things to avoid

Jargon, hedging, and corporate throat-clearing. Never blame the customer for a
problem Northwind caused.
