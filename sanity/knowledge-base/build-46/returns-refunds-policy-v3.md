# Returns & Refunds Policy v3

Owner: legal@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/legal/returns-v3.pdf
Effective: 2 March 2026

This policy is the authoritative statement of Northwind's returns and refunds
commitments. Where any other Northwind document disagrees with it, this document
governs.

## Return window

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.

The window runs from the date the carrier records delivery, not the date of
purchase. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by
proof of purchase. Northwind pays return postage for faulty goods; the customer
pays it for change-of-mind returns.

## Restocking fee

Opened items are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

The fee is deducted from the refund and is disclosed at the point the return is
authorised. It does not apply to faulty goods, to goods that differ from their
description, or to returns made under the EU cooling-off provisions.

## Exclusions

Perishable goods, personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned. This
exclusion does not affect a customer's statutory rights.
