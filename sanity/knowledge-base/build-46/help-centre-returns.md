# Help Centre — Returns

Owner: support@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/help/returns
Last reviewed: 2 March 2026

A plain-language summary of how returns work, written for customers rather than
for lawyers. The Returns & Refunds Policy is the governing document.

## How long do I have?

You have 30 days from delivery to start a return.

Start one from your order history, or reply to your confirmation email. You will
get a prepaid label if the item arrived faulty.

## What condition does the item need to be in?

Unused and in its original packaging. If you have opened it, a restocking fee may
apply — the exact amount is shown before you confirm the return.

## When do I get my money back?

Once the item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, the refund goes back to
the card you paid with.
