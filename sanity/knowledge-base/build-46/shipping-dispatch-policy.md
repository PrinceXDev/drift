# Shipping & Dispatch Policy

Owner: ops@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/legal/shipping.pdf
Effective: 21 June 2026

## Free shipping threshold

Orders over 75 USD ship free within the contiguous US.

Orders below the threshold are charged a flat rate calculated at checkout. Alaska,
Hawaii and international destinations are quoted separately.

## Dispatch time

Orders are dispatched within 2 business days.

Dispatch time is the interval between payment clearing and the parcel leaving the
warehouse. It excludes weekends and public holidays, and it is separate from
carrier transit time.

## Carriers

Northwind ships with regional carriers selected per destination. Tracking is
issued at dispatch.
