# Limited Warranty Terms

Owner: legal@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/legal/warranty.pdf
Effective: 4 November 2025

## Warranty term

All products carry a 24 month limited warranty.

The term runs from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials
and workmanship under normal use.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, cosmetic wear, consumable parts, and damage caused by
modification or by use outside the documented operating conditions.

## Making a claim

Contact support with proof of purchase. Northwind will repair, replace or refund
at its discretion. This warranty is in addition to, and does not affect, a
customer's statutory rights.
