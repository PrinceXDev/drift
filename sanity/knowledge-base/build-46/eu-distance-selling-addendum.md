# EU Distance Selling Addendum

Owner: legal@northwind.example
Canonical URI: https://northwind.example/legal/eu-addendum.pdf
Effective: 14 August 2025

This addendum applies to customers resident in the European Union and is
supplementary to the Returns & Refunds Policy.

## Cooling-off period

EU customers have a 14 day cooling-off period in addition to the standard window.

The cooling-off period runs concurrently with, and does not shorten, the standard
return window. No reason need be given and no restocking fee may be charged for a
return made within it.

## Refunds

Refunds under this addendum are issued to the original payment method, including
standard outbound delivery charges.
